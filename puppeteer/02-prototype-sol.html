HAND PUPPET / PROTOTYPE

Drag the numbered controls, or enable your camera.

1 thumb → left arm / 2 index → left leg / 3 middle → head / 4 ring → right leg / 5 pinky → right arm

Controls are anchored at the top. Moving fingers relative to neutral pose pulls the puppet strings. Camera and landmarks are mirrored horizontally at 50% opacity under the puppet.
